Privacy Policy

Would You Rather: Faith
Effective September 28, 2026

Information we collect

Would You Rather: Faith does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data with the developer.

Information stored on your device

Your card choices, app preferences, catalog data, and cached card translations are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this locally stored app data, subject to normal iOS backup and restoration behavior.

Apple Translation

When you select a dynamically translated language on a supported version of iOS, the app uses Apple's Translation framework. Translation is performed by Apple's operating-system service. iOS may download language resources and Apple may process diagnostic or service information according to Apple's privacy policy. The developer does not receive the text you translate or the resulting translations.

External links

Bible-reference buttons can open pages on Bible.com or another browser destination. Those websites operate under their own terms and privacy policies. The developer does not receive information about your activity on those sites.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy and effective date will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project's GitHub support page.
